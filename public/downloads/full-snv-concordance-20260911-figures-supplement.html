Supplement: genome-wide OpenSpliceAI and SpliceAI comparison

Abstract

We describe the scoring collection, annotation pairing, masking, and statistical summaries used to compare OpenSpliceAI with SpliceAI. Supporting analyses examine score differences, threshold transfer, output precision, annotation context, selected positions, and variation across genes and genomic blocks. All results use the same dated scientific snapshot as the main report; inference progress is documented separately.

This supplement accompanies the main report. AG, AL, DG and DL denote acceptor gain, acceptor loss, donor gain and donor loss, respectively. MAX is the largest of those four scores. Scientific results use the September 9 snapshot; separately dated progress observations do not change the analyzed populations.
