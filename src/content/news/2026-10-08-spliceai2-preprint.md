I'm honored to have been part of the SpliceAI2 team, and grateful to Kishore Jaganathan for the opportunity.

The preprint, [A unified framework for quantitative splicing and transcript prediction](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.08.757673v1), presents quantitative splice-site usage, splice-junction usage, and transcript prediction (also available as an [Illumina PDF](https://assets.illumina.com/content/dam/illumina-marketing/images/genomics-research/articles/spliceai2/SpliceAI2.pdf)). The [announcement](https://www.illumina.com/science/genomics-research/articles/introducing-spliceai2--the-next-generation-of-splicing-and-trans.html), [code and documentation](https://github.com/Illumina/SpliceAI2), [model weights](https://huggingface.co/illumina-ai/SpliceAI2), and [precomputed predictions](https://huggingface.co/datasets/illumina-ai/SpliceAI2-data) are available through the official release resources.

*Update (October 11, 2026): The preprint is now posted on [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.08.757673v1).*
