Illumina has launched **SpliceAI2**, with a [preprint I co-authored](/publications/spliceai2/) on quantitative splice-site usage, splice-junction usage, and transcript prediction. The [announcement](https://www.illumina.com/science/genomics-research/articles/introducing-spliceai2--the-next-generation-of-splicing-and-trans.html), [code and documentation](https://github.com/Illumina/SpliceAI2), [model weights](https://huggingface.co/illumina-ai/SpliceAI2), and [precomputed predictions](https://huggingface.co/datasets/illumina-ai/SpliceAI2-data) are available through the official release resources.
