Congratulations to Celine Hoh and the team on [SpliceCOV](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4425/17/10/1307), now published in *Genes*! SpliceCOV uses supervised learning to filter noisy splice sites and transcript boundaries from RNA-seq data, helping improve transcriptome assembly precision. [Explore the code](https://github.com/celinehohzm/SpliceCOV).
