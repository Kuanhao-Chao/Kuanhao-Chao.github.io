Our complete diploid human genome benchmark is published in [Cell](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.06.016). The benchmark covers 99.4% of the diploid HG002 genome and adds 701.4 Mb of autosomal sequence plus both sex chromosomes that earlier benchmarks left out. I contributed to the gene annotation, using [LiftOn](/publications/lifton/) to help identify additional gene copies on the maternal and paternal haplotypes. Data and code are on [GitHub](https://github.com/marbl/HG002).
