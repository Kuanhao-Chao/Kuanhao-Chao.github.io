It was an honor to take part in the Telomere-to-Telomere Consortium's Q100 project, whose complete diploid HG002 genome benchmark is published in [Cell](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.06.016). My contribution was to the gene annotation, using [LiftOn](/publications/lifton/) to help identify additional gene copies on the maternal and paternal haplotypes. Data and code are on [GitHub](https://github.com/marbl/HG002).
