abstract: >
  SpliceCOV uses supervised learning to distinguish RNA-seq-supported splice sites
  and transcript boundaries from noisy candidate features using coverage and
  splice-junction evidence. In the reported evaluations, supplying the filtered
  features to StringTie improved transcript-level precision while preserving
  transcript recovery.
